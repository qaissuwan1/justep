📋 Pharmacology · Beta Blockers
A 58-year-old man with stable angina reports cold extremities after starting a new medication. Which mechanism is most likely?
A. Activation of α2-adrenergic receptors
B. Blockade of β1-adrenergic receptors
C. Inhibition of ACE
D. Blockade of calcium channels
💡
Select an answer to see
the full explanation
📖 Explanation
Beta blockers competitively block β-adrenergic receptors. β1 blockade → ↓ HR & contractility. Cold extremities = β2 blockade → peripheral vasoconstriction.
⚠️ Board trap: Non-selective agents (propranolol) worsen asthma. Use cardioselective (metoprolol) in COPD/asthma.

Mechanism of action of warfarin?
✓ Answer
